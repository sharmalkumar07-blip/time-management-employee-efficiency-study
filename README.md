# A Study on Time Management as a Tool for Employee Efficiency in Tiruvannamalai

## Overview
This research project examines how effective time management influences employee efficiency and productivity.

## Author
S. Sharmal Kumar

## Institution
Loyola College, Vettavalam

## Degree
Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA)

## Objectives
- Analyze the importance of time management.
- Evaluate employee efficiency.
- Study workplace challenges and productivity.

## Methodology
- Sample Size: 50 Respondents
- Research Type: Descriptive Study
- Area: Tiruvannamalai District

## Key Findings
- Time management improves productivity.
- Poor planning is a major challenge.
- Work stress affects efficiency.
- Organizational support improves performance.
